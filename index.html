About Me

I am a research assistant at the Institute of Molecular Biology, Academia Sinica (IMB), with a background in Biomedical Science from National Chung Cheng University. My research training began in Dr. Michael W. Y. Chan’s lab, where I studied the anticancer effects of wogonin in ovarian cancer through the AMPK–TET2–5hmC axis and gained experience in cancer biology, epigenetic regulation, and molecular biology experiments.

Currently, I work in Dr. Jun-An Chen’s lab at IMB, where I am involved in a project investigating how non-coding RNAs regulate gene expression. Through this experience, I have been learning molecular biology techniques, imaging-based assays, and tissue-based experiments, while developing a stronger interest in RNA-mediated gene regulation and RNA–protein interactions.

I am particularly interested in how non-coding RNAs, RNA–protein interactions, and epigenetic mechanisms contribute to gene expression regulation in different biological contexts. I hope to continue building my experimental and conceptual foundation through graduate-level research training.
