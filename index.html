Biography

Profile

Profile

I graduated from the Department of Biomedical Sciences at National Chung Cheng University and currently work as a research assistant in Dr. Jun-An Chen’s laboratory at the Institute of Molecular Biology, Academia Sinica.

My current work involves research on Meg3 lncRNA, ELAVL family proteins, and long neuronal gene regulation. I am especially interested in how RNA biology, RNA-binding proteins, and molecular regulatory mechanisms shape gene expression in cellular contexts.

My interest in this direction began during my IMB summer internship, where I studied Meg3–ELAVL2-mediated alternative splicing using a mini-GFP splicing reporter. During my undergraduate research training in Dr. Michael W. Y. Chan’s laboratory, I worked on ovarian cancer biology and epigenetic regulation, examining EMT-related gene expression after wogonin treatment. Together, these experiences shaped my research interests in molecular and cell biology, gene regulation, and RNA biology.
