About Me

I am a research assistant at the Institute of Molecular Biology, Academia Sinica, with a background in Biomedical Science from National Chung Cheng University. My research training spans molecular biology, cancer biology, RNA-mediated gene regulation, imaging-based assays, and animal work.

I am particularly interested in mechanistic questions that connect molecular regulation to cellular phenotypes.

I enjoy troubleshooting experimental systems and refining protocols to resolve mechanistic ambiguity.

Interests
